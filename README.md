### 🔬 About Me
* 🌱 I'm a **Lecturer** in the Department of Pathology at **Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University (SAU)**, Dhaka, Bangladesh (Jan 2026 – present). I hold an **MSc in Pathology** (CGPA 3.95 / 4.00) and a **BSc in Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry** (CGPA 3.74 / 4.00), both from SAU.
* 🦠 My research covers **viral and bacterial diseases of livestock** (PPRV, FMDV, LSDV), **antimicrobial resistance**, **One Health** and disease epidemiology.
* 📈 I use **spatiotemporal analysis and machine learning** to study disease patterns and climate links, and **systematic reviews and meta-analyses** to synthesize global evidence.
* 💻 I'm building my **bioinformatics** skills in RNA-seq, genomic data analysis and R.
